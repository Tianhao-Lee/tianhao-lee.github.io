## Artificial Intelligence for Computational Biology

My research focuses on machine learning methods for biological data analysis. Current topics include:

- **Single-cell Omics** — computational methods for cell type annotation, representation learning, and single-cell data analysis.
- **Spatial Transcriptomics** — models that integrate spatial context with molecular profiles to study tissue organization.
- **Deep Learning** — representation learning, self-supervised learning, and generative models for high-dimensional biological data.
- **Multi-omics Integration** — methods for learning shared and modality-specific information across molecular measurements.
