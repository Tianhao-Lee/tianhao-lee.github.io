I am a Ph.D. student at the College of Computer Science, Sichuan University, working on artificial intelligence for computational biology. My research develops machine learning methods that read, integrate, and complete the molecular profiles of individual cells.

#### Current research

- **Cell type annotation** — Labels are scarce, sequencing noise is heavy, and cell types are long-tailed and keep growing, so a model trained on one dataset rarely transfers cleanly to the next. I approach this from three angles: self-supervised pre-training with parameter-efficient fine-tuning, cross-omics knowledge alignment for heterogeneous multi-omics fusion, and distribution- and expression-aware incremental learning for continuously expanding cell type sets.
- **Single-cell gene completion** — Recovering an entire gene column when it is absent from an expression profile and no neighbourhood information is available to interpolate from. I build generative frameworks for this setting.
- **Spatial transcriptomics** — A new direction now in progress. There will be more to read here once the work is ready.

#### How I work

Representation learning, self-supervised learning, and generative models, applied to high-dimensional biological data with heavy noise and scarce labels.
